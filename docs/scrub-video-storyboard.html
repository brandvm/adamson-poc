What's in the render

The three parts and their details

1 · Waveguide housing (front)

  • Thick ring body with eight flat sides and rounded corners, dark bronze anodised finish.
  • Square mounting tab sticking out on its left side.
  • Six bolt holes around the front rim.
  • Inner opening with a stepped lip, filled by a gold woven mesh dome that bulges toward camera.
  • Moves toward camera-left. It's the part that travels furthest.

2 · Motor (middle)

  • Short polished cylinder; the front face is spun metal with fine circular brushing and a small raised centre cap.
  • A chamfered edge, then a wider flange ring at the back with screws.
  • A short steel coupling shaft with two grooves points at the waveguide's tab.
  • A red signal connector with a steel barrel sits on its right side.
  • Barely moves; everything separates around it.

3 · Mounting plate (rear)

  • Flat plate with eight sides and rounded corners, darker than the housing.
  • Large round opening with a stepped seat for the motor flange.
  • Ring of small mounting holes, and two long rounded pockets milled into the right side.
  • Moves back, to camera-right.

Delivery

File spec

  • Length: 8 s at 30 fps (240 frames). No sound.
  • Desktop: 1920 × 1080, product centred; the product stays centred at every point; text sits in the top-left corner, then the bottom-right corner, so keep those corners clear.
  • Mobile: 1080 × 1350 (4:5), same motion, camera pulled back so all three parts fit; text sits at the bottom on phones.
  • Background: pure black #000, no vignette edge.
  • Camera: locked. The driver itself turns from face-on to the 3/4 view (6–24% of the scroll), then holds that angle. No cuts.
  • Motion: after the turn, parts travel only along the driver's axis with no further spin; each move eases in and out. Hold the first 6% and the last 14%.
  • Last frame matches the poster hero/hero-speaker.png, which reduced-motion visitors see instead of the video.
  • Encoding: every frame a keyframe so seeking is smooth: ffmpeg -i in.mov -c:v libx264 -g 1 -crf 20 -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart -an out.mp4
